Share your videos to every account you manage — from one place.

SocialFlow is a publishing tool for creators. Link your YouTube channel, connect the Facebook Pages, Instagram professional accounts and TikTok accounts you own, and publish a post to all of them in one campaign.

Connect your accounts

Sign in with Facebook, Instagram or TikTok using each platform's official login. SocialFlow never asks for your social media passwords.

Write once

Compose a post with your video or link, preview it, and choose which of your accounts should receive it.

Publish safely

Posts are sent one by one with spacing between them, and you can pause or cancel at any time. Each destination shows its delivery status.

How SocialFlow uses TikTok

  • Login Kit (user.info.basic): you choose to connect your TikTok account; SocialFlow shows its display name and avatar so you can recognise it.
  • Content Posting API (video.publish): when you launch a campaign that includes your TikTok account, SocialFlow uploads the video you selected to your TikTok account, with the caption you wrote.
