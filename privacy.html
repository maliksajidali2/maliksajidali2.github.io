Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

SocialFlow ("we", "us") is a tool that lets a user publish their own content to the social media accounts they manage. This policy explains what data SocialFlow handles and why.

Information we collect

  • Your SocialFlow account: email address and a securely hashed password, used to sign you in.
  • Connected social accounts: when you choose to connect Facebook, Instagram or TikTok through their official login, we receive an access token and basic profile information (account ID, display name, username, profile picture). For Facebook we also receive the list of Pages you manage and their Page access tokens.
  • Content you create: campaign text, links, and images or videos you upload, plus the delivery status of each post.
  • YouTube channels you link: the public channel ID and the public list of your channel's videos.

We do not collect your social media passwords, your followers' data, direct messages, or analytics.

How we use it

  • To publish the posts you create, to the accounts you select, at the time you choose.
  • To show you which accounts are connected and whether each post succeeded.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone except the platform you are publishing to (Meta or TikTok), which receives the content you chose to post.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database. Social access tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage and are only used by the SocialFlow server. Uploaded media is stored so the platforms can retrieve it for publishing.

Retention and deletion

  • Removing a connected account in SocialFlow (Accounts → Remove) deletes its tokens and its destinations immediately.
  • You can revoke SocialFlow's access at any time in your Facebook, Instagram or TikTok settings.
  • To delete your SocialFlow account and all associated data, email maliksajidale@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.

TikTok data

With your permission, SocialFlow uses TikTok's user.info.basic scope to show your TikTok display name and avatar, and video.publish to upload videos you select to your TikTok account. TikTok data is used only for these purposes.
